## Design rules
Use these layouts as the visual direction: panoramic scenic heroes, strong heading hierarchy, compact cards, outlined icons, tags, alternating dark and light sections, and scenic calls to action. Adapt blue accents to the approved purple Home palette. Preserve the approved laptop landing transition and realistic featured-project scenes unless Justin requests a change.

Mockup copy and facts are illustrative. Verified portfolio content and report-backed case-study findings take precedence. Do not adopt unsupported dates, metrics, coursework, projects, or achievements. Keep missing personal details and results as explicit placeholders. Do not serve entire mockups as website content or claim generic office images show actual employers.
